# Blindspot: video scripts

## ⚠️ Length rules (checked on each hackathon's page)
| Hackathon | Video limit |
|---|---|
| Remote Control AI | **2–3 minutes max** |
| Next Byte V5 | **1–3 minutes** |
| Arbiter Hacks V1 | **2–3 minutes max** |
| LaunchPad Hacks | about 2–3 minutes (encouraged) |

**Submit Script A (2:55) everywhere.** Script B (about 7 minutes) is the full tour. Use it only as an extra "full walkthrough" link (LaunchPad allows a recorded walkthrough) or for practice. Never submit a video over 3 minutes where the limit is 3.

---

## Before you record (10 minutes)
1. **Wake the server.** Open https://blindspot-8lr1.onrender.com **2 minutes before recording**. The free plan sleeps and takes about 50 seconds to wake.
2. **Have the sample PDF ready:** `harbor_coffee_order_report.pdf`, the one I sent you. Put it on your Desktop.
3. **Have the deck open** (Blindspot_deck.pdf or .pptx) in presentation mode in a second window.
4. Browser: close other tabs, zoom to **90%** (Cmd and −), use full screen, dark mode is fine.
5. In Canva: **Record yourself → Screen + camera** (or screen only), a quiet room, and a test of the mic first.
6. **Read slowly.** About 150 words a minute is the pace these scripts are timed for.

**Legend:** 🎬 = what to show or click · 🎙️ = what to say

# SCRIPT A: 2 minutes 55 seconds (submit this one)

### 0:00 – 0:15 · Hook
🎬 Slide 1 (title).
🎙️ "Your sales data is hiding something. Your totals tell you *what* happened, never *why*. This is **Blindspot**, the AI analyst for small online stores."

### 0:15 – 0:35 · Inspiration and the real problem
🎬 Slide 2 (Inspiration), then Slide 4 (The real problem).
🎙️ "Every small shop can see how much it sold. Almost none can see why. And that blind spot is expensive. One in five new businesses closes in its first year, and winning a new customer costs five to twenty-five times more than keeping one. The warning signs are already in your sales. Nobody is reading them."

### 0:35 – 0:45 · Totals lie
🎬 Slide 5 (Totals lie).
🎙️ "In a real UK shop, sales were *up* five percent, while its best-seller fell fifty-one percent and forty-two top customers quietly walked away."

### 0:45 – 1:20 · Live demo: any file
🎬 Switch to the browser → click **Open the app** → drag `harbor_coffee_order_report.pdf` into **Drop your export here** → the file appears in the list → click **Next**.
🎙️ "Watch. This is a sixty-eight-page PDF sales report, the kind of file most tools can't read. Blindspot takes CSV, Excel or PDF, from Shopify, Etsy, Amazon, Square, Tableau, in any language."
🎬 The report opens. Point at the column cards, then at "What Blindspot cleaned up".
🎙️ "It shows its work: every column it found, and every row it fixed. Twenty cancelled orders left out, twenty-one refunds counted as returns. No guessing."
🎬 Click **Looks right**.

### 1:20 – 1:50 · The answer
🎬 Overview. Point at the green ▲ growth number on the **Sales** card (about +16%), then **Top findings**.
🎙️ "Sales are up sixteen percent. Looks great. But Blindspot found what the total hides: the coffee subscription is down seventy percent, and eight of the best customers stopped ordering. Each problem is ranked by the money at stake, with a health score and what to fix first."

### 1:50 – 2:25 · How AI is used
🎬 Click **AI Analyst** → click the suggested question **"Why did revenue change?"** → wait for the answer → scroll to **Ideas, checked by math**.
🎙️ "Here's how AI is used: **AI suggests. Math checks.** Ask anything in plain English. Claude picks from fifteen read-only analysis tools and explains the real numbers. It even proposes new business ideas, but every idea is tested with statistics before you see it. The numbers come from the tools, not the model."

### 2:25 – 2:45 · Proof
🎬 Click **Source Data** (the four **Match** badges), then show Slide 13 (Proof, not promises).
🎙️ "Every number is traceable to the exact rows. Seven of seven headline numbers match an independent check, and a hundred eighty-seven automated tests pass."

### 2:45 – 2:55 · Close
🎬 Slide 19 (Stop guessing. Start knowing.), then Slide 20 (Try it live + QR).
🎙️ "Stop guessing. Start knowing. See it, prove it, fix it. Blindspot. Try it live today."

**Word count: about 400 (2:55 at a calm pace).** If you run long, cut the Source Data part (2:25–2:45) first.

# SCRIPT B: about 7 minutes (full tour, optional)

### 0:00 – 0:30 · Hook (Slide 1)
🎙️ "Every small shop has a blind spot. Not because the owner isn't working hard, but because the numbers don't explain themselves. Your totals tell you what happened. They never tell you why. I'm going to show you **Blindspot**, an AI analyst that reads your sales and tells you exactly what's costing you money."

### 0:30 – 1:05 · Inspiration (Slide 2)
🎙️ "Every small shop can see how much it sold. But owners can't answer the questions that actually matter. Which customers stopped coming back? Which product is quietly dying? What should be fixed first? They don't need more data. They need someone to read it. So I built Blindspot."

### 1:05 – 1:45 · The problem (Slides 3 and 4)
🎙️ "Small shops fly blind. Totals hide the problems, a data analyst costs over seventy thousand dollars a year, and dashboards show numbers without the reason behind them."
🎬 Slide 4.
🎙️ "And it matters. About one in five new businesses closes in its first year, and about half by year five. Winning a new customer costs five to twenty-five times more than keeping one, and keeping just five percent more customers can raise profit by twenty-five to ninety-five percent. **What you can't see can sink you.**"

### 1:45 – 2:10 · Totals lie (Slides 5 and 6)
🎙️ "Here's a real UK online shop with two years of sales. The total says up five percent. But underneath, its best-selling product fell fifty-one percent, forty-two of its best customers quietly left, about three hundred thousand pounds a year, and fewer new buyers came back."
🎬 Slide 6.
🎙️ "Your totals tell you *what*. Blindspot tells you *why*. **Don't guess. Assess.**"

### 2:10 – 2:30 · How it works (Slide 7)
🎙️ "Five steps. Upload your data. Blindspot cleans it, finds the problems across your products, countries and best customers, proves them with statistics, and ranks the fixes by money."

### 2:30 – 3:30 · Live: upload any file
🎬 Browser → **Open the app**.
🎙️ "This is the start page. You can upload an export, connect Shopify live, or explore our sample store."
🎬 Click **Shopify** under "How to export from" for 3 seconds (the guide), click back, then drag `harbor_coffee_order_report.pdf` into the box.
🎙️ "Every platform has a step-by-step export guide. Now I'll drop in a real-world headache: a sixty-eight-page PDF report. The file waits in a list. I can add more or remove one with the X. Nothing is read until I press Next."
🎬 Click **Next**.
🎙️ "Blindspot rebuilds the table from the PDF and shows its work. Here's what each column means. If anything were wrong, I'd fix it in one click. Here's everything it cleaned: cancelled orders left out, refunds booked as returns, page labels skipped. And here's what this file unlocks: trends, products, customers. Profit is locked because the file has no product costs. Add a costs file and it unlocks."
🎬 Click **Looks right**.

### 3:30 – 4:20 · Overview
🎬 Overview, slowly from top to bottom.
🎙️ "Sales are up sixteen percent. A total like that makes you relax. Blindspot doesn't. It compares with the same months last year, so a holiday rush can't fake growth. The **health score** shows how much is at risk. **Fix this first** is the single biggest problem. **Top findings** are ranked by money at stake: eight best customers stopped ordering, and the coffee subscription is down seventy percent while the business grew. **What's driving the change** splits growth into more orders versus bigger orders. **What to do about it** turns each problem into an action."
🎬 Top bar: open the currency picker → type **USD** → pick it.
🎙️ "Selling abroad? Switch to any of thirty currencies and every amount converts at today's rate."

### 4:20 – 5:10 · AI Analyst
🎬 **AI Analyst** → click **"Why did revenue change?"** → show the answer → point at **Agentic workflow** → click **AI ideas** (or scroll to **Ideas, checked by math**).
🎙️ "This is where AI comes in. **AI suggests. Math checks.** I ask in plain English. Claude decides which of fifteen read-only analysis tools to call, runs them, and explains the real results. You can see every tool it used. Then I ask for new ideas. Claude proposes business theories, and Blindspot tests each one with statistics: supported, not supported, or not enough evidence. **Ideas from AI. Proof from math.** The numbers come from the tools, not the model."

### 5:10 – 5:50 · Decide what to fix
🎬 **Risk Intelligence** (point at the risk map), then **Scenario Simulator** (move one lever, e.g. Keep more customers), then **Executive Reports**.
🎙️ "Risk Intelligence maps every problem by money and how it was proven: top right means fix first. The Simulator answers what-if: keep a few more customers, and here's the profit. And the one-page report checks ten areas of the business, with a thirty, ninety and hundred-eighty-day roadmap you can send to a partner or investor."

### 5:50 – 6:15 · Trust
🎬 **Source Data**.
🎙️ "And you never have to take it on faith. Every row it analysed is here, searchable, downloadable, and the totals match the dashboard exactly. **Showing your work builds trust.**"

### 6:15 – 6:45 · How it's built and proof (Slides 12 and 13, briefly 14)
🎙️ "Under the hood: React and TypeScript in the browser, Node on the server, my own math engine kept separate from the AI so results are fast, free and repeatable, and Claude with thirty-four typed tools, also available over MCP. Seven of seven numbers match an independent Python check, a hundred eighty-seven automated tests pass, and Shopify access is read-only. Your data stays yours."

### 6:45 – 7:05 · Challenges, learned, next (Slides 15, 16, 18, quickly)
🎙️ "The hardest parts were messy real data, PDFs that don't store tables, and a free server with five hundred twelve megabytes. I learned that AI is best when it's checked. Next: more live connectors, weekly alerts, and comparing your store with similar shops."

### 7:05 – 7:20 · Close (Slides 19 and 20)
🎙️ "Every sale tells a story. Blindspot reads them all. **Stop guessing. Start knowing.** Scan the code and try it live."

## If something goes wrong while recording
- **The site is slow to load:** the server was asleep. Wait 50 seconds, then refresh.
- **The AI answer is slow:** keep talking over it ("Claude is choosing which tools to run…"), or cut that part in Canva.
- **You stumble:** keep going and trim it in Canva. Nobody sees the cuts.
